## Pitch (large h2)
Local missionary practitioners will walk along side you in a community of practice as you learn biblical principles and implement global best practices in the diverse Los Angeles Metro. Participants will be trained to share the gospel, make disciples, start churches, and train leaders to do all of the Great Commission. Whether you plan to move overseas or live domestically, one will gain experience on how to implement and execute a comprehensive strategy.

## FAQs
**Q: Who is this for?**
A: Anyone who is excited about seeing disciples and churches started for the sake of reaching people for Jesus. We are looking for people that have a learning posture and are willing to be stretched.

**Q: How long is it?**
A: The cohort is designed for those who will commit to 15 weeks with the option to continue for an additional 15 weeks.

**Q: What is the time commitment?**
A: Cohort Gathering (2-3hrs) – Encourage and equip one another.
Harvest (2hrs) – Entry, gospel, disciple a group or church in the harvest.
Train (2hrs) – Train other Christians to do the same.
Accountability – (1-2 hrs per month)
Personal Abiding – (1 hour per day)

**Q: Where will the cohort be?**
A: Greater Los Angeles Metro but the gathering place will be at Neighbors and Nations Church in Buena Park.

**Q: Who is facilitating the equipping?**
A: Local missionary practitioners with years of experience representing different organizations like e3 Partners (e3partners.org), Here to There Movement (htmovement.com) and churches that have a passion and a vision to see the Great Commission accomplished in our lifetime.

**Q: How much does it cost?**
A: $50, however you get it back if you have perfect attendance.
