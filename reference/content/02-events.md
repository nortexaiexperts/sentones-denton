# Upcoming Disciple Making Trainings & Missions Events
Sent Ones hosts disciple-making trainings, missionary gatherings, mobilization events, and summits designed to equip believers to engage the lost, share the gospel, make disciples, form healthy churches, and develop multiplying leaders. Explore upcoming opportunities across North America and beyond to get equipped, connect with other practitioners, and take your next step in the Great Commission.
