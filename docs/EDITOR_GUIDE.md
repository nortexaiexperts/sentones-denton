# Updating the Sent Ones Denton website

A one-page guide for editors.

## Where to go

**https://sentonesdenton.sanity.studio**

Sign in with the ministry account (sentonesdenton@gmail.com). Works on a computer or phone; nothing to install.

## The golden rule

Nothing changes on the website until you click the green **Publish** button. About **two minutes** after you publish, the change is live on sentonesdenton.org. Refresh the page to see it.

- To **change** something, click the existing item in the list and edit it.
- The **+** button always creates a **new** item.

## Add an event

1. Left menu: **Upcoming events** → click **+**.
2. **Basics** tab:
   - **Event name**
   - **Web address**: click **Generate**
   - **Type of event**: Training, Mobilization, Harvest or Worship
   - **Starts** and **Ends**: date and time in Central Time. For a multi-day event, pick the last day as the end.
   - **Show on the home page**: leave on for important events
3. **Where** tab: venue name, street address (leave empty for private homes), city. Or tick **This is an online event**.
4. **Details** tab:
   - **Event image**: drag in a landscape photo, then fill in **Describe the photo** (one short sentence)
   - **Short summary**: one or two sentences for the list
   - **Full description**: schedule, what to bring, who it's for
   - **Registration link**: paste the Eventbrite link if there is one
   - **Cost**: optional
5. Click **Publish**.

Past events move to **Past events** by themselves. There's no need to delete them.

## Change or cancel an event

- **Change:** open it, edit, **Publish**.
- **Cancel:** open it, then use the small arrow next to **Publish** → **Delete**. It disappears from the website after the rebuild.

## Standing rhythms

Regular gatherings (like the Denton County Strategy Meeting) live under **Standing rhythms**. Fill in the name, **How often** ("Second Thursday of each month"), time, where, a short description, and optional bullet points. **Display order** controls which shows first: lower numbers come first.

## Blog posts

1. If the writer is new, add them first under **Authors** (name, role, short bio) and **Publish**.
2. **Blog posts** → **+**. Fill in title, web address (**Generate**), author, date, at least one category, main image with its description, a two-sentence excerpt, and the article.
3. In the article, use **Heading** for section titles, **Quote** for pulled quotes, and the link button for links. You can drop images straight into the text.
4. **Publish.**

## If something looks wrong

- **Red message on a field:** something required is missing. The message says what.
- **Change not showing after five minutes:** refresh the website with Ctrl+F5. If it's still missing, contact your web team. Your content is safe in the dashboard; only the website copy is behind.
- **Made a mistake:** open the item and use its history (top right of the item) to see earlier versions and restore one, then **Publish**.

## What still goes through the web team

Page text, the MTC page, resources, photos on the main pages, the menu, and anything about design. Send those requests to your web team.
