## How to review

1. **Open the link on your phone first.** Most visitors will see the site on a phone. Tap through the menu and scroll every page.
2. **Then open it on a computer.** Same pages, wider screen.
3. **For each page, answer the three questions in the table below.** Short notes are perfect. "Love it" is a valid answer.
4. **Only add a screenshot when words aren't enough** (for example "this photo" or "this paragraph"). How to take one:
   - iPhone: press the side button and volume-up together.
   - Android: press power and volume-down together.
   - Windows: press Windows + Shift + S, drag over the area.
   - Mac: press Shift + Command + 4, drag over the area.
   Paste the screenshot under the matching row.
5. **Mark how important each change is:** *Must change before launch*, *Would be nice*, or *Just a thought*.

Forms on the preview are switched off; you can fill them in to see how they look, but nothing is sent.

## Specific questions we need answered

These decide what we build next, so a one-line answer to each helps a lot.

1. **Name.** Is "Sent Ones Denton" the right name, and how do you describe your relationship to Sent Ones (chapter, partner, affiliate)?
2. **Permission.** Has Sent Ones confirmed we may use their logo, trailer footage, values text and training library?
3. **Contact.** Which public email (and phone, if any) should appear on the site?
4. **Coaching calls.** Should "discovery call" and "coaching call" go to the national Sent Ones booking page, a Denton booking link, or the contact form?
5. **Homepage headline.** Keep "Lost people matter to God, and so they must matter to us", or use something of your own?
6. **Values.** Keep the four "We are…" statements as written on sentones.com?
7. **Photos.** Do you have photos of real Denton gatherings we can use, with permission from the people in them?
8. **Events.** Any confirmed dates we can put in now?
9. **MTC.** Which of these are decided: residential or not, housing and meals, dates, length, cost, scholarships, who leads it?
10. **Website address.** Do you already own a domain name you want to use?
